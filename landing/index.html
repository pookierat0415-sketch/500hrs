Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. Hours, logs, and photos are saved in the app's storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to an account. Export your report regularly so you always have a copy.

Does it need internet?

Only for the optional AI polisher. Everything else, including the timer, logs, photos, and PDF and Word exports, works with no connection. Notes waiting to be polished are processed automatically once you're back online.

What happens to my notes when I use the AI polisher?

It's off until you turn it on in Settings. When it's on, the text of an entry is sent to Google Gemini to be rewritten. Photos and hours are never sent, and your original notes stay saved so you can switch back.

My school has its own report format. Will it work?

Yes, if it's a Word document. Add placeholders like {name}, {totalHours}, or a {#weeks}…{/weeks} section where your logs should go, upload it in the Report tab, and download it filled in. The app includes a sample template and a full placeholder guide.

My OJT needs 300 or 600 hours, not 500.

The progress ring currently uses a 500-hour goal. Every hour you log is still recorded and exported exactly, so your report totals are correct whatever your requirement is.
